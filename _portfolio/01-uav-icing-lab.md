**Role:** Director

Research group at NTNU's Department of Engineering Cybernetics working on atmospheric in-flight icing of unmanned aircraft: icing wind tunnel experiments, CFD simulations of ice accretion and performance penalties, ice protection systems, ice detection, and adverse-weather mission planning.
